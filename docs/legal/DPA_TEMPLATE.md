## 3. ROLES & RESPONSIBILITIES

### 3.1 Controller Responsibilities
BA MABULANE (PTY) LTD shall:

1. **Lawful Basis Determination**
   - Ensure lawful basis exists for all personal information processing (POPIA Sec 11)
   - Maintain records of consent where required (especially POPIA Sec 26 for sexual-life data)

2. **Transparency & Consent**
   - Provide privacy notices to data subjects before processing
   - Obtain explicit consent for Special Personal Information (POPIA Sec 26)
   - Maintain records of consent timestamps, IP addresses, and policy versions

3. **Instruction & Control**
   - Issue written, documented instructions to the Operator
   - Limit Operator processing only to instructions provided
   - Authorize sub-processors only after notice and opportunity for objection

4. **Data Subject Rights Support**
   - Respond to data subject access/correction/deletion/objection requests
   - Require Operator to provide information necessary to fulfill rights requests

5. **Audit & Inspection Rights**
   - Conduct periodic audits of Operator's compliance
   - Request and review security and processing documentation

6. **Breach Notification**
   - Assess breach incidents and determine whether notification is required
   - Notify South African Information Regulator and affected data subjects within 72 hours where required (POPIA Sec 22)

### 3.2 Operator Responsibilities
[Operator Name] shall:

1. **Process Only Per Instructions**
   - Process personal information only on documented, written instructions from Controller
   - Not use data for own purposes or outside defined scope
   - Refuse processing requests that appear unlawful

2. **Confidentiality & Staff Compliance**
   - Ensure all staff with access are bound by confidentiality agreements
   - Maintain personnel training records on data protection obligations
   - Restrict access to minimum necessary personnel

3. **Security Measures**
   - Implement technical and organizational security measures per POPIA Schedule 1 requirements:
     - Encryption at rest and in transit
     - Access controls and authentication
     - Audit logging and monitoring
     - Regular security assessments and penetration testing
     - Incident response procedures
   
4. **Data Integrity & Availability**
   - Maintain data accuracy and completeness
   - Implement backup and recovery procedures
   - Provide Business Continuity/Disaster Recovery documentation

5. **Sub-Processor Management**
   - Not engage sub-processors without prior written approval from Controller
   - Ensure sub-processors execute equivalent DPA obligations
   - Maintain and share list of authorized sub-processors

6. **Breach Notification**
   - Immediately (within 24 hours) notify Controller of any suspected or confirmed breach
   - Provide reasonable cooperation in breach investigation and remediation
   - Preserve forensic evidence and logging records

7. **Data Subject Rights Support**
   - Provide Controller with information necessary to fulfill data subject requests
   - Execute deletion/correction requests within agreed timeframes
   - Support Controller in responding to Subject Access Requests (SARs)

8. **Transparency & Cooperation**
   - Provide audit reports, certifications (SOC2, ISO 27001, etc.) upon request

## 5. SECURITY & COMPLIANCE

### 5.1 Technical Safeguards
Operator shall implement and maintain:
- **Encryption:** AES-256 or equivalent for data at rest; TLS 1.2+ for data in transit
- **Access Controls:** Role-based access control (RBAC), principle of least privilege, multi-factor authentication for admin access
- **Audit Logging:** Immutable logs of all access, modifications, and deletions
- **Intrusion Detection:** Real-time monitoring and alerting for unauthorized access attempts
- **Vulnerability Management:** Regular patching, penetration testing, and security assessments

### 5.2 Organizational Safeguards
Operator shall implement and maintain:
- **Personnel Training:** Annual data protection and security training for all staff with data access
- **Confidentiality Agreements:** All staff must execute written confidentiality agreements
- **Background Checks:** Where legally required/feasible for staff with access to sensitive data
- **Access Restrictions:** Only authorized personnel may access personal information

### 5.3 Special Handling - Sexual-Life Data
If Operator processes sexual-life data (POPIA Sec 26 Special Personal Information):
- Enhanced encryption and access controls mandatory
- Strictly limited access (e.g., only when user explicitly activates sexual features)
- Immediate deletion upon user opt-out or account termination
- Cannot be used for profiling, marketing, or secondary purposes

### 5.4 Compliance Certifications
Operator shall provide (upon request):
- SOC 2 Type II audit report (or equivalent)
- ISO 27001 certification (if available)
- Annual security assessment summary
- GDPR/data protection compliance attestation (where applicable)

### 5.5 Incident Response Plan
Operator shall maintain and share:
- Written incident response procedures
- Target response time: acknowledge breach notification within 24 hours
- Forensic preservation procedures
- Post-incident review and lessons learned documentation

## 6. DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS & DELETION

### 6.1 Access Requests (POPIA Sec 34)
- Operator shall provide Controller with data subject information within **10 business days**
- Operator acknowledges Controller's obligation to provide SARs to users within **30 days**

### 6.2 Correction & Deletion (POPIA Sec 35)
- Upon Controller instruction, Operator shall correct or delete personal information within **7 days** (or agree to alternative timeline)
- Operator shall provide confirmation of deletion within **3 days**

### 6.3 Objection & Restriction (POPIA Sec 36)
- Operator shall comply with Controller instructions to restrict processing of objected data
- Operator shall document restrictions and enforce them across all systems/backups

### 6.4 Verification Data Handling (Age Verification Providers Specific)
- Raw ID documents, selfies, and liveness data shall be deleted within **24 hours** of successful verification (unless legal hold applies)
- Operator shall retain only: verification outcome (pass/fail), timestamp, and verification method
- Operator shall certify deletion completion via written confirmation

### 6.5 Post-Termination Retention
- Upon DPA termination, Operator shall:
  1. Delete all personal information within **30 days** (or per Controller's written instruction)
  2. Provide written certification of deletion
  3. Retain no copies except where legally required (with Controller written approval)

### 7.1 For Age/Identity Verification Providers

**Verification Data Inputs:**
- SA ID number
- ID document image
- Selfie
- Liveness detection data
- Date of birth / age calculation

**Verification Data Outputs:**
- Pass/Fail status
- Timestamp
- Method used (document/liveness/selfie)

**Retention:**
- Raw media (ID photo, selfie, liveness video): delete within **24 hours** of verification completion
- Verification outcome metadata: retain per Controller's instruction (typically 6-12 months for audit purposes)
- No secondary use: data cannot be used for marketing, profiling, or sold to third parties

**Compliance Obligations:**
- Comply with POPIA Schedule 1 security standards
- Provide evidence of POPIA compliance and security certification
- Maintain audit trail of all verification requests and outcomes
- Support Controller's age-verification audit requirements (e.g., random sample audits)

- Billing address

**Payment Data Outputs:**
- Transaction confirmation
- Payment receipt reference
- Settlement report
- Fraud/risk assessment (risk score only, not detailed reasoning)

**PCI DSS Compliance:**
- Processor must maintain PCI DSS Level 1 or Level 2 certification
- No raw payment card data shall be stored by Controller

**Retention:**
- Transaction records: retain per South African tax requirements (5 years minimum)
- Fraud risk assessments: retain for chargeback dispute resolution (up to 540 days)
- Personal information (email, phone, address): retain only as long as account active + 12 months

**Compliance Obligations:**
- Provide PCI DSS audit report upon request
- Maintain fraud prevention and chargeback procedures
- Support Controller's subscription/cancellation workflows per CPA Section 14

## 8. AUDIT & INSPECTION RIGHTS

### 8.1 Audit Schedule
- **Scheduled Audits:** Controller may conduct annual security/compliance audits with **15 business days' notice**
- **Unscheduled Audits:** In case of suspected breach or non-compliance, Controller may request immediate audit with **3 business days' notice**
- **Regulatory Audits:** Operator shall cooperate with South African Information Regulator investigations without unreasonable delay

### 8.2 Audit Scope
- Technical security controls and encryption effectiveness
- Access control implementation and audit logging
- Staff training records and confidentiality agreements
- Sub-processor compliance and data handling
- Breach incident logs and remediation records
- Data retention and deletion procedures

### 8.3 Audit Costs
- Controller-initiated audits: costs borne by Controller (unless non-compliance found)
- Compliance verification audits (e.g., SOC 2): Operator shall provide reports; Controller may request independent verification at Operator's cost if significant findings arise

### 8.4 Remediation
- Operator shall address audit findings within agreed timeframe (default: 30 days for high-severity, 90 days for medium/low)
- Controller may suspend data transfer to Operator if critical security gaps persist after 30-day grace period

## 9. TERMINATION & POST-TERMINATION

### 9.1 Termination Triggers
This DPA may be terminated by either party:
1. **Mutual agreement** in writing
2. **Controller termination for cause** (material breach, non-compliance, inadequate security) with **30 days' written notice**
3. **Without cause** by Controller with **90 days' written notice**
4. **Operator termination** with **180 days' written notice** (subject to Controller's consent to transition period)

### 9.2 Post-Termination Obligations
Within **30 days** of termination:
1. Operator shall provide Controller with copy of all personal information in structured, portable format (or Controller shall retrieve it)
2. Operator shall securely delete all personal information copies
3. Operator shall provide written certification of deletion
4. Operator shall return or destroy any confidential information belonging to Controller

### 9.3 Survival
Sections addressing confidentiality, liability, and data security shall survive termination for **12 months**.
