# Subscription Terms (CPA + ECTA)

**Last Updated:** 2026-09-02  
**Status:** Draft pending legal review

## 1. Plans
- Free Plan: Basic functionality
- Premium Monthly: **R99** (incl. VAT)
- Premium Annual: **R999** (incl. VAT), saving approximately 16% compared to monthly billing

## 2. Shared Premium Model
A couple account has two linked users. If either partner purchases Premium, Premium access applies to both linked user identities and expires simultaneously.

## 3. Billing Providers
Payments are processed by third-party operators, including:
- Ozow (EFT)
- Paystack (Card/EFT)

## 4. Auto-Renewal
Premium subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each billing period unless cancelled before renewal.

- A renewal reminder is sent approximately 5 days before charge date
- Users can cancel any time from the dashboard
- Cancellation prevents future renewals; current paid period remains active until period end

> **Legal review required:** CPA Section 14 fixed-term and auto-renewal treatment for this model.

## 5. Cooling-Off and Refunds
Where applicable, users may exercise cooling-off rights within 5 business days under ECTA Section 44, subject to service-usage limits and legal exceptions.

If Premium features have not been materially used during the cooling-off window, a refund may be granted.

## 6. Price Changes
For subscription price increases, notice is provided at least 40 business days in advance before the new price takes effect.

## 7. Failed Payments
If payment fails:
- Premium may be suspended or downgraded to Free
- Retry logic and grace windows may apply
- Access fully restores once payment succeeds

## 8. Taxes
Displayed prices include VAT unless otherwise stated. Users remain responsible for any personal tax obligations not collected at checkout.
