## 5. Special Personal Information (POPIA Section 26)
Sexual life data is treated as **Special Personal Information**.

We process this data only where **both partners** separately provide explicit opt-in consent (for example, via an optional checkbox for Men’s/Women’s/Mixed Circles).

Consent records include:
- Timestamp
- IP address
- Consent text/version accepted

Either partner may withdraw consent at any time. On withdrawal, sexual-feature processing stops and the account is downgraded to non-sexual features.

> **Legal review required:** POPIA Section 26 consent flow and wording.

## 6. Purpose of Processing
We process personal information to:
- Verify age (18+)
- Authenticate a two-person couple account
- Provide communication and intimacy platform features
- Process subscription payments
- Track referrals (30-day attribution window)
- Prevent fraud and abuse

## 7. Lawful Basis
