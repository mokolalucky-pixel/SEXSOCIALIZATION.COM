# Referral Terms (CPA Section 43 Considerations)

**Last Updated:** 2026-09-02  
**Status:** Draft pending legal review

## 1. Program Nature
This is a single-level affiliate referral program.

- It is **not** multi-level marketing (MLM)
- It has no downlines, tiers, or recruitment levels
- Commission is earned only on directly referred paying couple accounts

> **Legal review required:** confirm compliance with CPA Section 43 and anti-pyramid provisions.

## 2. Commission Structure
Commission is 25% of qualifying net subscription payment (subject to final tax/accounting treatment):

- Monthly plan reference amount: **R24.75**
- Annual plan reference amount: **R249.75**

Net treatment (VAT and gateway fee treatment) must be finalized by legal/accounting advisors.

## 3. Tracking and Attribution
Referrals are tracked using:
- Referral URL parameter (`?ref=CODE`)
- 30-day local attribution storage/cookie
- Last-click attribution model

## 4. Qualification Rules
A referral qualifies only if:
1. Referred couple completes both Partner A and Partner B verification
2. Referred couple becomes Premium
3. Premium remains active for 14 days (cooling/chargeback protection window)

## 5. Payout Terms
- Minimum payout threshold: **R250**
- Payout method: EFT
- Payout timing: within 30 days of valid payout request
- Enhanced verification (including FICA controls) may be required for high-value payouts (e.g., >R5000)

## 6. Taxes and Withholding
Where applicable, withholding may be applied (example: 10%). Referrer is responsible for personal tax declaration and compliance.

> **Legal/accounting review required:** confirm withholding basis and invoicing requirements.

## 7. Prohibited Practices
The following may result in forfeiture and removal from program:
- Self-referrals
- Spam or unsolicited promotions
- Misleading/false advertising
- Brand impersonation
- Any unlawful marketing practice

## 8. Affiliate Disclosure
Referrers must clearly disclose affiliate relationships in promotional content, in line with CPA advertising principles (including Section 29 considerations).

## 9. Program Changes
We may amend or discontinue the referral program with reasonable notice.
