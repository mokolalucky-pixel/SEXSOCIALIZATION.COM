## Important Notice

This document is an operational draft and **not legal advice**. Final wording and enforceability must be confirmed by a qualified South African attorney.

## 1. Program Definition

- Single-level affiliate referral model
- Not MLM
- No recruitment/downline tiers

## 2. Commission

- 25% of qualifying net payment (final tax/VAT treatment subject to attorney/accountant confirmation)
- Monthly reference commission: **R24.75**
- Annual reference commission: **R249.75**

## 3. Tracking

- Referral format: `?ref=CODE`
- 30-day local attribution storage
- Last-click wins

## 4. Qualification Criteria

Referral qualifies only when:
1. Both partner verifications are completed
2. Couple subscribes to premium
3. Premium remains active for at least 14 days (anti-fraud/chargeback window)

## 5. Payout Terms

- Minimum payout threshold: **R250**
- EFT payout target: within 30 days of valid request
- Enhanced KYC/FICA checks may apply on high-value payouts (example: >R5000)

## 6. Tax

Potential withholding may apply where legally required; each referrer remains responsible for declarations and personal tax compliance.

## 7. Prohibited Behavior

- Self-referrals
- Spam
- False/misleading claims
- Brand misuse
- Unlawful marketing conduct

## 8. Disclosure Duty

Referrers must clearly disclose referral relationships in line with CPA advertising fairness requirements.
